Profile

I act as the interface between the people defining the need and the people building and operating the system.

My role is to set direction for products based around storage and GPUs. I question specifications and headline benchmarks when the workload, failure behaviour or operational cost could change the choice. The aim is not to turn product management into engineering; it is to make sure the product decision reflects what the system can actually deliver.
